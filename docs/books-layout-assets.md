# Books layout asset

The Books reading-desk background was generated with the built-in imagegen tool and saved to `public/assets/images/books-reading-background.png`.

Final prompt:

> Generate one photorealistic website background asset, wide 3:1 landscape. Overhead pale white marble reading desk. A cup of coffee toward the upper right, a small green leafy plant entering the top right corner, an open book partly cropped along the lower right edge. Large softly textured empty white space across left and center so product covers and headings can be overlaid. Natural diffuse light, subtle shadows, quiet realistic editorial photography, green accents. No text, no logo, no UI, no book cover graphics. Save image for use as a bookshop section background.

The page uses real published books only. Special offers require an actual discount; no artificial sale countdown is displayed. The children’s shelf uses categories containing Children, Kids or Watoto. Test cover illustrations used during local browser checks are not published or stored as books.
